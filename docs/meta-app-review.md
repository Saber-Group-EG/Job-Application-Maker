## Allowed usage: descriptions to paste

**pages_show_list**
> SG Hiring (ats.sabergroup-eg.com) is an applicant tracking system for companies. A company admin connects the company's Facebook Page with Facebook Login for Business from Company settings → Facebook & Instagram. We use pages_show_list to list the Pages the admin manages and chose to share, so they can see which Page is connected and choose where the company's job openings are posted. We store only the Page id, name and Page access token, and the admin can disconnect at any time, which deletes them.

**pages_read_engagement**
> Required together with pages_manage_posts. After the admin connects, we read the Page's basic information (id, name, linked Instagram account) and receive the Page access token used to publish. After publishing a job post we read the post id to show the admin a "View" link to the published post. We do not read comments, followers or insights.

**pages_manage_posts**
> When a job opens for applications, SG Hiring publishes it to the company's own Facebook Page: the job title, company, location, deadline, a short description and a link to the job's application form. This happens automatically only if the admin switched on "Auto-post on the Page" for that Page, or when a recruiter clicks "Post now" on a job (Jobs → Promote). Each job is posted once; the recruiter sees the status and a link to the post. We only create posts; we never edit or delete posts the company made.

**business_management**
> Many companies own their Page and Instagram account through a Meta business portfolio. Facebook Login for Business needs business_management so the admin can pick Pages and Instagram accounts that belong to their business portfolio when connecting. We use it only to read which business assets the admin shared with SG Hiring; we never change business settings, people or assets.

**instagram_basic**
> Used to read the Instagram business account linked to the connected Facebook Page (account id and username), so the admin sees "@account" in Company settings and can switch on Instagram posting for it.

**instagram_content_publish**
> When a job opens, SG Hiring publishes a square job image (job title, company, "Apply now") with a caption (job details and the application link) to the company's own Instagram business account. This happens automatically only if the admin turned on "Auto-post on Instagram", or when a recruiter clicks "Post now" for Instagram on a job. The image is generated by SG Hiring from the job's own details.

**public_profile**
> Used to show which person connected the company's Facebook Page ("Connected by <name>") in Company settings.

### WhatsApp descriptions (already pasted in the form)

**whatsapp_business_messaging**
> SG Hiring sends WhatsApp messages only from its own business number ("SG Hiring") using approved templates: login, two-step and password reset codes for users who ask for them, and updates to applicants about their own application (received, interview scheduled, offer sent, missed interview). Applicants give their number on the form and agree to updates; companies choose automatic or manual per update. No marketing messages.

> Used to manage SG Hiring's own WhatsApp Business Account: read its phone number and status, list and create the message templates we send and check their approval, and subscribe our app to the account's webhooks for delivery status and replies. ## Data handling: suggested answers (check with whoever handles legal)

- **Data processors / service providers with access to Platform Data:** Yes.
  - MongoDB Atlas (database hosting): stores Page ids, names, Instagram account ids and usernames, and encrypted Page access tokens.
  - Render (application hosting): runs the server that uses the tokens to publish.
  - Cloudflare R2 (file storage): stores the job images we generate (no Meta data).
- **Responsible entity for Platform Data:** Saber Group, Egypt (use the legal name shown in the business verification).
- **Requests from public authorities for Platform Data in the last 12 months:** No.
- **Policies for such requests:** choose the options that apply. Typically: we review the legality of requests, and we provide only the minimum data needed.
- **Data security:** Page access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and never sent to the browser. All traffic uses HTTPS. A company can disconnect at any time, which deletes its Pages and tokens. Data deletion instructions are at https://sabergroup-eg.com/policies?tab=data-deletion.
